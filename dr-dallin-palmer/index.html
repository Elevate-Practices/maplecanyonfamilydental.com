Dr. Dallin Palmer, DMD

His focus is on you, the patient.

Book online(801) 491-8191

After high school, Dr. Dallin Palmer served a two-year mission in Italy for his church, and later met his wife. They were married in 2004 and have two wonderful children.

Dr. Palmer graduated from the Kornberg School of Dentistry at Temple University in Philadelphia, where he earned his DMD. He had the privilege of traveling to Haiti to provide dental treatment to hundreds of people who otherwise had no access to medical or dental care.

He has always felt a strong desire to help others, which greatly influenced his choice of dentistry. Dr. Palmer enjoys all areas of general dentistry, including fillings, crowns and bridges, extractions, root canals, dentures and implants, and he is Invisalign certified.

His focus is on you, the patient. He's committed to giving you an enjoyable, comfortable visit and high-quality care that fits your needs.

  • DMD, Kornberg School of Dentistry, Temple University
  • Invisalign certified
  • Dental care for hundreds of people in Haiti

Taking new patients of all ages.
