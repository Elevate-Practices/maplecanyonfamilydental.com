Teeth Whitening

How professional whitening lifts stains safely.

Book online(801) 491-8191

Press play with sound for the narration

What the video covers

  1. Here's what to expect with professional teeth whitening.
  2. Over time, coffee, tea, red wine, tobacco, and simply getting older can leave teeth looking yellow or dull.
  3. First, we make sure your teeth and gums are healthy. Fillings and crowns don't whiten, so we'll plan around them.
  4. There are two great ways to whiten. In-office whitening gives you fast results in one visit, and custom trays let you whiten at home on your own schedule.
  5. A professional whitening gel is applied to your teeth, and it gently lifts stains from deep within the enamel.
  6. Your teeth get noticeably brighter. Some people feel mild sensitivity, but it usually fades within a day or two.
  7. To keep your smile bright, rinse after coffee or tea, keep up with your regular cleanings, and do touch-ups as needed.
  8. If you have any questions along the way, just ask. Your dental team is always happy to help.
