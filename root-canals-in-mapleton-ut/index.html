When you need one

When the soft inside of a tooth gets infected or inflamed, it can cause a strong toothache, swelling or sensitivity that lingers. A root canal treats the infection and saves the tooth.

How it works

  1. We numb the tooth
  2. We clean out the infected pulp
  3. We seal the inside of the tooth
  4. Usually a crown follows to protect it

After treatment

Most people feel much better within a few days. Mild soreness is normal and usually eases with over-the-counter pain relievers.

Common questions

Are root canals painful?

The goal is to stop the pain. With numbing, it often feels a lot like getting a filling.

Do I need a crown afterward?

Often, yes, especially on back teeth. We'll let you know.
