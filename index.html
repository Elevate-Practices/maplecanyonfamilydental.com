Questions we hear a lot

Are you taking new patients?

Yes. We see kids, teens and adults. A new-patient cleaning, exam and X-rays is $129 ($89 for kids 11 and under). Book online or call (801) 491-8191.

Do you take my insurance?

We accept most dental insurance plans, including Delta Dental, Cigna, MetLife, Aetna, Guardian and Blue Cross Blue Shield. We'll file your claims and help you get the most from your benefits.

I don't have insurance. What can I do?

Join the Maple Canyon Membership: two cleanings, exams, X-rays and free whitening each year, plus 20% off other care, starting at $249 a year.

I have a toothache. What should I do?

Call us at (801) 491-8191. A toothache exam is $49, and it's free when you have the treatment done with us.

What are your hours?

Monday through Friday, 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM. We're closed Saturday and Sunday.
