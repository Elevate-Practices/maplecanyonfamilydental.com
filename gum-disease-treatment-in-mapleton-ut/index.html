What gum disease is

Gum disease is an infection of the gums and the bone around your teeth. Early on, gums may bleed when you brush. Left alone, it can lead to loose teeth.

Signs to watch for

  • Gums that bleed when you brush or floss
  • Red, puffy or tender gums
  • Bad breath that won't go away
  • Gums pulling away from teeth
  • Teeth that feel loose

How we treat it

  1. We measure your gums and take X-rays
  2. A deep cleaning removes buildup below the gumline
  3. We check how your gums heal
  4. Regular cleanings keep it under control

Common questions

Can gum disease be reversed?

Early gum disease (gingivitis) often can, with good cleanings and home care. Later stages can be controlled with treatment.

Is a deep cleaning painful?

We numb the area so you stay comfortable.

How often will I need cleanings?

Often every three to four months at first. We'll set a schedule that fits your gums.
